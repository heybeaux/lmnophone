# Play Store opening fixes

- Turning the screen off closes the Play Store without waiting for the screen to wake.
- A refused opening says “This didn't open. Try again.” A failed phone check still says “Not ready”.
- With no openings left, the phone shows “No openings left for now” straight away.
- Opening counts use “1 opening” when one remains.
- “Add an app” explains that it opens the Play Store after the wait.
- The opening timer runs only while an opening or its wait still needs it.

Google sign-in is unchanged. There is still no time-left notice over the Play Store.
