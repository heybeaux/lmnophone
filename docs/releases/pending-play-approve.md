# Add an app after Play

After a Play Store opening, you can add a new app right away, before the original
10 minutes run out. Each app is checked against the app and signer recorded when
it was installed. You choose what it can use before adding it to Home.

Returning to LMNOPhone closes the Play Store and shows the new apps. **Done** or
Back on these screens ends the opening; it does not open another approval step.
Restarting LMNOPhone also ends it. Apps you did not add stay hidden. You can use
**Add a hidden app** later with a fresh PIN.

Installing from the Play Store needs a Google account on the phone. This increment
is for self-managed phones. Settings openings and supervised approvals are not
included. Emulator checks use test apps without signing in to Google.
