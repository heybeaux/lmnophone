LMNOPhone screens: black and white

Mockups for PLA-36. Font Lexend (400 body, 500 names and titles). Black, white and greys only, from Generosity Catalyst's palette. Tokens and rules: docs/ux/design-tokens.md. Words are the current flow copy (copy-test fixes from PLA-12; product decisions from PLA-14). Names as in the copy-test packet: Margaret uses the phone, Sam looks after it, Alex is the helper.

Frames are 412 × 915 dp, the size of the spare moto g power 5G, except frame 10 (360 × 640 dp, the smallest supported screen).

The 200% frames (3 and 10) use the sizes Android 15 gives text at 200% (its non-linear font scale table), but they are drawn in a browser, so they are illustrative. The real check is on the phone, in the MVP build and its final review.
