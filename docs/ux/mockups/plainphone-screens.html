Plainphone screens: black and white

Mockups for PLA-36. Font Lexend (400 body, 500 names and titles). Black, white and greys only, from Generosity Catalyst's palette. Tokens and rules: docs/ux/design-tokens.md. Words are the current flow copy (copy-test fixes from PLA-12; product decisions from PLA-14). Names as in the copy-test packet: Margaret uses the phone, Sam looks after it, Alex is the helper.

Each frame is 412 × 915 dp, the size of the spare moto g power 5G. Dashed boxes on the Emergency close-up are measurements, not part of the screen.
