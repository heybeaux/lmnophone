Turn LMNOPhone off?
This makes your phone an ordinary Android phone again. Every app will open, including web browsers and the Play Store, and the home screen will look different.
Photos, messages and contacts are kept. Nothing is erased.
It happens after at least 24 hours of the phone being on. Until then, you can cancel it. Nothing can make it shorter.
This test build doesn't send texts.
To use LMNOPhone again later, the person who set up LMNOPhone on this phone has to set it up again with a computer.
LMNOPhone only turns off this way, with a PIN. A flat battery doesn't turn it off: when the phone starts again, LMNOPhone is back.
