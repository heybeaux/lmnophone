What’s new in this version
PIN settings has Add an app. After a 10-minute wait, the Play Store opens for 10 minutes. New apps stay hidden until you add them under Add a hidden app. Up to 2 openings a day; these limits can't be changed yet.
The Play Store needs a Google account before it can download apps. This phone isn't signed in to one yet.
OK
While the Play Store is open, nothing on screen shows the time left. Going back to LMNOPhone ends it.
If an app from the Play Store is removed and installed again, LMNOPhone may say it isn't ready to hand back. Restarting the phone may clear this.
Help and privacy
This is a test version. Parts that are not ready yet are not shown. For phone settings that need a PIN, open More, then PIN settings.
Unsent voice messages stay on this phone for up to 7 days. Sending, deleting, replacing a recording, removing its person or turning Family off erases the recording.
LMNOPhone has no account, analytics or server of its own.
