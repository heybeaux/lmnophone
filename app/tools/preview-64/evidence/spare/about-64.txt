Help and privacy
This is a test version. Parts that are not ready yet are not shown. For phone settings that need a PIN, open More, then PIN settings.
Unsent voice messages stay on this phone for up to 7 days. Sending, deleting, replacing a recording, removing its person or turning Family off erases the recording.
LMNOPhone has no account, analytics or server of its own.
